About the property

Description property

Dixon Residential Estate Agents are delighted to present 23 The Green to the Navan property market.

This four-bedroom (or three-bedroom plus office) home is ideally located in the highly sought-after Bakers Hall estate.

Upon entering, you are welcomed by a spacious entrance hall leading to a bright and airy living room overlooking the front of the property. To the rear, there is an open-plan kitchen/dining area with access to a convenient utility room. A guest WC and understairs storage complete the ground floor accommodation.

Upstairs, the property comprises three well-proportioned bedrooms and home office. The primary bedroom benefits from a modern en-suite and large built-in wardrobes, while a stylish family bathroom completes this level.

Externally, the property features a large cobble-locked driveway. Additional benefits include side access leading to a sunny rear garden. The low maintenance rear garden boasts a Barna shed, and is further enhanced by, an electric socket, and a water tap.

Baker Hall is ideally positioned to enjoy the best of both convenience and connectivity. Situated just a short drive from Dublin, the development offers easy access to the M3 and R147, making it an excellent choice for commuters. Regular bus and rail services provide convenient connections to Dublin City Centre, while Navan’s excellent transport links make travelling further afield straightforward. The location is particularly appealing for families and those seeking everyday convenience, with a host of amenities within walking distance, including local shops, schools, public transport links, Johnstown Shopping Centre and Navan town centre.

This A-rated home offers an excellent blend of comfort, convenience and modern living, making it an ideal choice for a variety of buyers seeking a well-connected home within easy reach of Dublin.
